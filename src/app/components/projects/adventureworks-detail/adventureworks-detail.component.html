This project analyzes four years of AdventureWorks bicycle company sales data to answer five core business questions: seasonality, growth trends, margin volatility, category profitability, and discount impact. The analysis revealed that margin losses are driven not by weak demand but by pricing failures — thousands of lines sold below cost with zero discount recorded.
